# Storefront (customer) surface brief

Scope: the public storefront: `/`, `/shop`, `/shop/[productId]`, `/account`, `/account/[mode]`, `/account/receipts/[saleId]`, the coming-soon state. Mode: Persuade on `/`; Operate on shop, product and account. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; no online payment claims; owner pinned a dark, premium aesthetic (2026-10-24 brief).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is the store's register receipt, printed at night. Store head, fuel, aisles, items and the customer's own receipts are itemised lines (name at one end, value at the other) on a dark counter. It refuses the hero-plus-card-grid shop page and the previous light "shelf tag" paper look.
OWN-WORLD: A near-black counter ground tinted a few degrees toward the store's logo hue, with thermal-white ink and one lit-sign accent in the logo hue, used only for the open dot, the primary action and the current aisle. Doto (dot-matrix) sets the store name, prices and totals; Sofia Sans Semi Condensed sets everything else. Receipts end in a torn edge, and items are joined to their price by dotted leaders. Selected states are solid and the rest are outlined (raised from the sewing-pattern challenger).
STORY: The visitor learns at once whether the store is open, where it is and today's fuel prices. They search or pick an aisle, read sizes and prices as receipt lines, and (signed in) see their real receipts and points in the same form.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The store name in dot-matrix across the full width (about 9vw). Under it, four small blocks on a 12-column grid: live open status, address with directions, phone, and fuel. Then a wide search line with the Search action and a Shop link. The itemised aisles start inside the first screen.
FORM: Night receipt, number 6 on my ordered list (1 lit box sign, 2 cooler-door wall, 3 fuel pylon, 4 door-glass hours decal, 5 neon OPEN sign, 6 thermal register receipt, 7 scratch ticket). seed:595a19ba98b1d417. Raises: palette discipline of three inks only (depot blind); the receipt column is the layout grid (elbow panel); a dot-matrix face built on one grid (Crouwel specimen). Signature interaction: the home receipt lines feed out top-down once on load, like a printer (reduced motion: no travel).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
