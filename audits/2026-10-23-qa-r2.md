- **Area:** dashboard

### C-028: Promotions can't be created; every save fails with "Unable to complete the request"
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/pricing` → Promotions → Configure promotion
- **Steps:** I tried three variants, each with Item discount, Percentage and "Percentage (basis points)" = 1000:
  - (a) "QA promo A draft": status Draft, target Kettle Chips;
  - (b) "QA promo B dated": status Active, min quantity 1, starts 2026-10-23 00:00;
  - (c) "QA promo C category": the same, but targeting the Snacks & Pantry category.
- **Expected:** "Promotion saved", and the promotion appears under 3 · Apply promotion at POS.
- **Actual:** Each one redirects with `?error=Unable to complete the request. Please try again.` and nothing is saved (`promotions` list is empty). Promotions are a day-one need (2-for-$5, 10% off) and never worked in round 1 either, which only opened the form. Because the error is masked, the merchant can't tell what's wrong.
- **Likely file:** `features/platform/actions.ts:1087` `configurePromotionAction` → `features/keystone/mutations/catalogOperations.ts:480` `configurePromotion`. Check the Railway log for the real exception. All of the visible validation rules were satisfied: status, rule type, name, basis points, dates, limits and store. Also change the field to a percent input (10 → 1000 bp), as was done for money.

### C-029: An approved store day silently goes stale when sales continue after approval
- **Area:** dashboard (Store Day)
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/reconciliation`, `/dashboard/platform/reports`, `/dashboard/platform`
- **Steps:**
  1. Round 1 generated and approved store day 2026-10-23 at 6:24 PM ($17.07 net).
  2. At 10:56 PM, the same trading day, ring a $5.17 sale and refund $1.61.
  3. Look at Reconciliation, Reports and Overview.
- **Expected:** The approved day is flagged as out of date, or the late activity raises an exception or is blocked until the day is reopened.
- **Actual:**
  - Reconciliation still showed "2026-10-23 Reconciled · 0 exceptions · $17.07 net". "Days to review 0", "Exceptions 0".
  - Overview meanwhile said "Sales today $22.24".
  - Only re-running "Generate close package" by hand reopened the day as In review at $20.63.
  - It went stale again after my later $5.39 sale, which is how it's left.
  - A real store that approves at shift change and keeps selling ends up with approved books that don't match the till, and nothing says so.
- **Likely file:** the store-day generation and approval in `features/keystone/mutations/*storeDay*`, and `features/platform/reconciliation/*`. Raise an operational exception when a sale, refund or cash movement lands in an approved or locked period, or refuse it for locked days.
