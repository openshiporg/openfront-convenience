## Blocker

### QA-001 — Every storefront product page returns 500, so nobody can add anything to a basket
- **Area:** storefront
- **URL:** https://openfront-convenience-production.up.railway.app/shop/cmvli2lsx000xkb0x8ynrmuk7 (and all 7 product URLs, including the QA product `/shop/cmvlinz9d004qkb0x96jnfdyd`)
- **Steps:** Open /shop. Click any product card, or load a product URL directly.
- **Expected:** the product page, with options, quantity and "Add to basket".
- **Actual:**
  - HTTP 500 and "This page couldn't load · Reload · Back". Console: `Minified React error #31 … object with keys {type, content}`.
  - The "Add to basket" form exists only on this page, so the basket and checkout can't be reached with items. The whole online-ordering path is dead.
  - Both client navigation and direct loads fail, at both widths.
- **Cause:**
  - `features/storefront/screens/ShopPages.tsx:172` renders `{product.description}` as text.
  - `features/keystone/mutations/storefrontOperations.ts:46` returns the raw Keystone `document` JSON (`description ?? ""`), and it is always truthy.
  - Onboarding writes descriptions in ProseMirror shape `{type:"doc",content:[…]}` (`features/keystone/mutations/onboardConvenienceBusiness.ts:410-418`), not Keystone's document format. That shape is the `{type, content}` object React rejects.
  - The QA product made through Pricing → Catalog items, with no description, also 500s, so even an "empty" description is not falsy.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Render the description with `@keystone-6/document-renderer`, or flatten it to plain text in `storefrontOperations`.
  - Write descriptions in Keystone's document format (see QA-002).

## Major

### QA-003 — The home page's main button, the account "Sign in" button and every aisle title are dark-on-dark and unreadable
- **Area:** storefront
- **URLs:** `/` ("Shop the store" button and the 5 aisle signs), `/account` signed out ("Sign in"), `/basket` and `/checkout` when empty ("Shop the store")
- **Steps:** Open the home page at either width.
- **Expected:** "Shop the store" in light text on the ink pill. Aisle names in white on the deep-brown sign.
- **Actual:**
  - The primary pill is a solid black lozenge with no visible label. The computed `color` and `background` are both `lab(7.19 …)`.
  - Aisle names ("Coffee Bar", "Cold Drinks" …) render near-black on dark brown.
  - These are the main calls to action on the home page, the account page and both empty states.
- **Cause:** `features/storefront/shop.css:58` `.sf a { color: inherit; }` (specificity 0,1,1) beats `.sf-btn { color: var(--sf-ground) }` (line 91) and `.sf-aisle__sign { color:#fff }` (line 183), which are both 0,1,0. So every `<Link className="sf-btn">` and `.sf-aisle__sign` inherits the ink colour. The `--signal` and `--quiet` variants hide the problem only where their colour happens to match.
- **Fix direction:** lower the specificity of the reset (`:where(.sf) a`), or raise the specificity of the link-button rules.

### QA-006 — The seeded catalog doesn't track inventory, so stock pages are empty, sales never reduce stock, and item search can't find products

### QA-013 — Tax rates can't be fractions of a basis point, so NYC's 8.875% is rejected
- **Area:** onboarding / dashboard
- **URLs:** onboarding dialog → Standard tax (%); `/dashboard/platform/pricing` → Tax → "rateBasisPoints"
- **Steps:** Enter 8.875 as the standard tax rate.
- **Expected:** accepted. 8.875% is the combined New York City sales tax, and the store's default timezone is America/New_York.
- **Actual:**
  - The browser blocks it: "Please enter a valid value. The two nearest valid values are 8.87 and 8.88" (`step="0.01"` in `OnboardingForm.tsx`).
  - The server stores integer basis points (`standardTaxBasisPoints: Math.round(percent*100)`), and the Pricing → Tax form takes integer `rateBasisPoints`.

- **Likely files:** `features/platform/onboarding/OnboardingForm.tsx`, `features/platform/onboarding/actions.ts`, the TaxRate model and the tax calculation. Store a rate with at least 3 decimal places of a percent, for example millibasis points or a decimal.
